#  AdaFruit USB to TTL Serial Cable

Putting this here because it drives me nuts trying to remember what pins go where.

| Signal | USB/TTL | Controller |
| ------ | ------- | ---------- |
| +5V | RED | 1 or 2 |
| GND | BLACK | 19 or 20 |
| RX | WHITE | 11 |
| TX | GREEN | 12 |

You can find a copy of the PDF data sheet
[here](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/1651403.pdf)
